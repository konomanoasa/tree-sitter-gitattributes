==================
Literal brace syntax
:cst
==================
{a,b} text
---

0:0  - 0:10   document
0:0  - 0:10     attribute_rule
0:0  - 0:5        pattern: pattern
0:0  - 0:5          glob_literal `{a,b}`
0:6  - 0:10       attribute
0:6  - 0:10         name: attribute_name
0:6  - 0:10           name_text `text`

==================
Escaped special markers
:cst
==================
\#\!\*\?\[
---

0:0  - 0:10   document
0:0  - 0:10     attribute_rule
0:0  - 0:10       pattern: pattern
0:0  - 0:2          escape `\\#`
0:2  - 0:4          escape `\\!`
0:4  - 0:6          escape `\\*`
0:6  - 0:8          escape `\\?`
0:8  - 0:10         escape `\\[`
